1. Summarize (2-3 sentences). Summarize the piece; this is extremely important for
the writer to understand what stood out about the work and if what they intended to
mean is actually understand by the reader. Is it in present, past, or future tense? What
is the speaker’s perspective? (first, second, third) Where does it take place? For poetry:
does it rhyme? Is it a particular form like a sonnet or villanelle?

2. Strengths. What are the strengths of the work on a craft level? How is dialogue,
characterization, setting, or description working well?

3. Feedback. What are the elements that need strengthening? After identifying the’writers strengths, where are some areas that could be revised for clarity, world-
building, expanding, etc.? Think of the elements of craft we’ve discussed and highlight
the strengths and weaknesses (setting, tension, dialogue, perspective, characters, plot,
etc.)
